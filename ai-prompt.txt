## 2. PHYSICAL INTERLOCKING & JOINERY MATHEMATICS (ZERO-GLUE PRECISION)

Every model in PuzzleCore is a REAL physical object cut from flat sheets (plywood, acrylic, or MDF) that snaps together with friction-fit mortise-and-tenon joinery without glue.

### A. The Joint as the Authoritative Single Source of Truth (SSOT)
Every mechanical connection MUST begin with an authoritative Joint definition in `ModelData.joints`:
$$\text{Joint} \longrightarrow \text{Connector} \longrightarrow \text{Receiver Derived from Connector} + \text{Clearance}$$

1. **NO INDEPENDENTLY AUTHORED SLOTS OR TABS**:
   - The AI must NEVER independently guess or hard-code matching tab and slot dimensions (e.g. authoring `tabWidth = 25` on Part A and `slotWidth = 20` on Part B).
   - Receiver cutouts MUST be derived directly from the joint connector definition:
     $$\text{Receiver Length} = \text{Connector Tab Width} + \text{clr} \quad (\text{typically } \text{width} + 0.10\,\text{mm})$$
     $$\text{Receiver Thickness} = t + \text{clr} \quad (\text{typically } t + 0.10\,\text{mm})$$
   - Any receiver cutout that does not match its corresponding Joint definition fails physical validation with `JOINT_GEOMETRY_NOT_DERIVED`.

2. **The Parametric Mortise Slot Rule (`cutouts`)**:
   All slots receptive to material thickness MUST be parametric using `(t || 3.0) + (clr || 0.10)`:
   ```javascript
   // HORIZONTAL SLOT (thickness along Y, slot length along X):
   { x: slotCenterX, y: slotCenterY, w: 20, h: (t || 3.0) + (clr || 0.10) }

   // VERTICAL SLOT (thickness along X, slot length along Y):
   { x: slotCenterX, y: slotCenterY, w: (t || 3.0) + (clr || 0.10), h: 30 }
   ```
   - `t`: Material thickness in millimeters (passed into `generate2D`, typically `3.0`, supporting `3.0`, `4.0`, and `5.0`).
   - `clr`: Kerf & friction-fit clearance tolerance (passed into `generate2D`, typically `0.10`).
   - **NEVER hardcode slot thickness to `3` or `3.0`!** Hardcoding causes physical validator errors when users change sheet thickness.
   - **Center-Based Local Coordinates**: In `cutouts`, `x` and `y` represent the **CENTER** of the cutout rectangle, NOT the top-left corner.
   - **Joinery Slots vs Decorative Openings**: The physical auditor evaluates all `cutouts`. To prevent joint looseness warnings, decorative features (doors, windows, surface patterns) should be drawn in `engravings` or marked with `{ arched: true }`. Dead receiver sockets without a matching declared joint are invalid.

### B. The Matching Tenon Tab Rule (`outline` extension)
Every tab that inserts into a mortise slot must match the Joint connector parameters:
1. **Tab Width**: Must equal the declared connector width:
   $$\text{Tab Width} = \text{Joint Connector Width}$$
2. **Tab Protrusion Depth**: Must equal material thickness $t$ (or $t + 0.2\text{mm}$ for positive flush fit):
   $$\text{Tab Depth} = t$$
   *(Example tab extending downward along bottom edge $y = -25$ from $x = -15$ to $+15$:)*
   `outline: [ [-25, -25], [-15, -25], [-15, -25 - t], [15, -25 - t], [15, -25], [25, -25], [25, 25], [-25, 25] ]`
3. **Harmonized Manufacturing Tolerances**:
   - **Fatal Hard Limits** (Below which wood grain shears or burns during manufacturing):
     $$\text{MIN\_EDGE\_MARGIN} = 1.5\,\text{mm} \quad (\text{fatal if } < 1.5\,\text{mm})$$
     $$\text{MIN\_SLOT\_SPACING} = 1.0\,\text{mm} \quad (\text{fatal if } < 1.0\,\text{mm})$$
   - **Recommended Durability Guidance** (Advisory best practice warning):
     $$\text{RECOMMENDED\_EDGE\_MARGIN} = 3.0\,\text{mm} \quad (1.0 \times \text{thickness})$$
   - Do NOT use 4mm or 5mm as a fatal threshold. Slots placed $\ge 1.5\text{mm}$ from the perimeter pass physical validation.

## 3. ZERO-COLLISION & OVERLAP PREVENTION (RIGID SOLID BODIES)

In real physical manufacturing, two laser-cut sheets cannot occupy the same volume.

1. **NO PENETRATING SOLID BODIES**:
   - Parts may ONLY touch where tenon tabs penetrate mortise slots, face-to-face abutting contact, or legitimate half-lap joints.
   - Solid plates must NEVER intersect or cross each other without a corresponding slot or notch cut into one or both parts!
2. **LOCALIZED JOINT COLLISION EXEMPTIONS (NO GLOBAL EXEMPTIONS)**:
   - Declaring a connection between two parts does NOT grant a global collision exemption for those parts!
   - Intentional penetration is permitted ONLY inside the actual world-space mating locus of the declared Joint (distance $\le$ joint mating radius).
   - Any solid penetration outside the declared joint locus triggers a fatal `COLLISION_OUTSIDE_JOINT_LOCUS` error.
3. **NARROW-PHASE 15-AXIS 3D OBB SAT COLLISION**:
   - Broad-phase AABB overlap detection is followed by exact narrow-phase 3D Oriented Bounding Box (OBB) Separating Axis Theorem (SAT).
   - Angled or rotated parts (e.g. $45^\circ$ pitched roof slopes) whose outer AABBs overlap in empty air are correctly separated by narrow-phase SAT.
4. **CORNER & T-JOINT THICKNESS OFFSETS**:
   - When two perpendicular walls meet at a $90^\circ$ corner, one wall must be inset or shortened by thickness $t$, or they must use alternating box/finger joints.
   - When a vertical wall rests on a horizontal base plate at $Y = 0$:
     - Base plate thickness spans from $Y = -t/2$ to $Y = +t/2$.
     - The solid body of the vertical wall must sit at $Y \ge +t/2$ (wall base shoulder at $Y = +t/2$).
     - Only the protruding tenon tabs should penetrate down into the base plate ($Y = -t/2$ to $+t/2$).
5. **AUTHORITATIVE 2D → 3D COORDINATE SYSTEM (ZERO CENTROID DRIFT)**:
   - The platform extrudes 2D geometry directly into 3D WITHOUT applying any X/Y centroid recentering (`geometry.center()` is strictly forbidden).
   - Any point defined at local 2D `(x, y)` retains its exact position in local 3D `(x, y)`.
   - Only the sheet thickness axis ($Z$) is centered around the mid-plane: $[-t/2, +t/2]$.
   - Asymmetric profiles (e.g. tabs only on the bottom edge) maintain their authored local origin with zero drift.

## 4. 3D POSITIONING & EULER ROTATION ALIGNMENT

Parts are positioned in 3D world space using parametric functions or vectors:
- `assembledPos: (t = 3.0) => [x, y, z]` (in millimeters, dynamically responsive to thickness)
- `assembledRot: [rx, ry, rz]` (in radians, Three.js Euler order 'XYZ')

### Standard Orientation Cheat-Sheet:
| Orientation | `assembledRot` | 2D Local $(X, Y)$ Maps To 3D World |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Horizontal Baseplate** (Flat on floor) | `[-Math.PI / 2, 0, 0]` | Local $X \to \text{World } X$, Local $Y \to \text{World } -Z$, Thickness $\to \text{World } Y$ |
| **Front / Back Wall** (Facing Z) | `[0, 0, 0]` or `[0, Math.PI, 0]` | Local $X \to \text{World } X$, Local $Y \to \text{World } Y$, Thickness $\to \text{World } Z$ |
| **Left Wall** (Facing -X) | `[0, Math.PI / 2, 0]` | Local $X \to \text{World } Z$, Local $Y \to \text{World } Y$, Thickness $\to \text{World } -X$ |
| **Right Wall** (Facing +X) | `[0, -Math.PI / 2, 0]` | Local $X \to \text{World } -Z$, Local $Y \to \text{World } Y$, Thickness $\to \text{World } X$ |
| **Horizontal Roof / Top Plate** | `[-Math.PI / 2, 0, 0]` | Local $X \to \text{World } X$, Local $Y \to \text{World } -Z$, Thickness $\to \text{World } Y$ |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **WORLD-SPACE JOINT ORIENTATION VALIDATION**:
> For every mechanical joint, the validator calculates connector width vector and receiver length vector in 3D world space. If they are perpendicular (dot product $< 0.25$), physical assembly is impossible and triggers `ORIENTATION_MISMATCH_90_DEG`.

### B. The Definitive Interlocking Joinery Cookbook & Coordinate Algebra

Follow these exact mathematical recipes to guarantee 100% physically valid, collision-free interlocking parts:

#### Recipe 1: Baseplate Floor-to-Wall Mortise & Tenon (The Inversion Rule)
When a horizontal baseplate has `assembledRot: [-Math.PI / 2, 0, 0]`, rotation around the X-axis by $-90^\circ$ causes an axis inversion:
$$\text{World } X = \text{Local } x$$
$$\text{World } Y = \text{Local } z \quad (\text{material thickness})$$
$$\text{World } Z = -\text{Local } y \quad \iff \quad \text{Local } y = -Z_{\text{world}}$$

> [!CAUTION]
> **THE BASEPLATE INVERSION RULE**:
> - If a Front Wall sits at **World $Z = +35$**, its receiver slot on the baseplate MUST be placed at **Local $y = -35$**!
> - If a Rear Wall sits at **World $Z = -35$**, its receiver slot on the baseplate MUST be placed at **Local $y = +35$**!
> *(Authoring $+35$ for World $+35$ places the slot 70mm away on the opposite side, triggering `COLLISION_OUTSIDE_JOINT_LOCUS`!)*

- **Receiver Slot Axis on Baseplate**:
  - For a wall running along World $X$ (width $W$ along $X$): The slot length is along Local $X$:
    $$\text{Slot Dimensions}: \quad w = \text{TabWidth} + \text{clr}, \quad h = t + \text{clr}, \quad \text{receiverAxis: 'X'}$$
  - For a wall running along World $Z$ (length $L$ along $Z$): The slot length is along Local $Y$:
    $$\text{Slot Dimensions}: \quad w = t + \text{clr}, \quad h = \text{TabWidth} + \text{clr}, \quad \text{receiverAxis: 'Y'}$$

#### Recipe 2: Wall Shoulder Elevation (Zero Floor Penetration)
A horizontal baseplate resting on the ground at $Y = 0$ has its top surface at $Y = +t/2$ (e.g. $+1.5\text{mm}$ for $3\text{mm}$ material).
For a vertical wall of height $H$ with 2D local contour centered at $y \in [-H/2, +H/2]$:
- Its bottom shoulder sits at local $y = -H/2$.
- To place the wall shoulder flush on top of the baseplate without sinking into solid wood:
  $$\text{assembledPos}_Y(t) = \frac{H}{2} + \frac{t}{2}$$
  *(Example: An 80mm tall wall with $t=3\text{mm}$ has $\text{assembledPos}_Y = 40 + 1.5 = 41.5\text{mm}$. The shoulder lands at $41.5 - 40 = 1.5\text{mm} = +t/2$, resting flush on the floor with ZERO solid penetration).*
- Only the tenon tab extending from $y = -H/2$ down to $-H/2 - t$ enters the baseplate mortise between $Y \in [-t/2, +t/2]$.

#### Recipe 3: Perpendicular Corner Wall Interlocking (Zero Corner Overlap)
When two perpendicular walls meet at a $90^\circ$ corner (e.g. Front Wall along $X$ at $Z = +35$, and Left Wall along $Z$ at $X = -35$):
- **Method A (Parametric Clearance Inset)**:
  Shorten the orthogonal wall so its edges stop flush before the intersecting wall's inner face:
  $$\text{Orthogonal Wall Width} = \text{Span} - t \quad (\text{or } \text{Span} - 2t \text{ if between two walls})$$
  *(Example: For a 40mm wide box with 3mm walls, the East Wall width is $40 - t = 37\text{mm}$ at $3\text{mm}$, $36\text{mm}$ at $4\text{mm}$, and $35\text{mm}$ at $5\text{mm}$. Parametric width guarantees zero corner collision across all thicknesses).*
- **Method B (Corner Mortise & Tenon)**:
  Left Wall features a tab protruding along $+X$ by depth $t$. Front Wall has a matching vertical slot at local $x = -35 + t/2$.
- **Method C (Alternating Box / Finger Joint)**:
  Along the vertical meeting edge, Wall A has protruding fingers at $y \in [0, 20]$, while Wall B has fingers at $y \in [20, 40]$.

#### Recipe 4: Pitched Roof Slopes & Apex Ridge Joinery
For two roof slopes of length $L$ and span $W$, pitched at slope angle $\pm\theta$ (e.g. $\theta = 45^\circ$, `Math.PI / 4`):
```javascript
// Left Roof Pitch:
assembledRot: [-Math.PI / 2, 0, Math.PI / 4],
assembledPos: (t = 3.0) => [-20 - (t / 2) * Math.sin(Math.PI / 4), 67.5 + (t / 2) + (t / 2) * Math.cos(Math.PI / 4), 0]

// Right Roof Pitch:
assembledRot: [-Math.PI / 2, 0, -Math.PI / 4],
assembledPos: (t = 3.0) => [20 + (t / 2) * Math.sin(Math.PI / 4), 67.5 + (t / 2) + (t / 2) * Math.cos(Math.PI / 4), 0]
```
- **The Normal Offset Vector**: Adding outward normal displacement $\pm(t/2)\sin\theta$ and $+(t/2)\cos\theta$ accounts for sheet thickness, preventing roof panels from intersecting gable walls or overlapping each other at the apex.
- **Apex Ridge Contact**: Declare apex joint as `type: 'contact'` with `connectionType: 'roof-ridge'` and `worldPos: [0, Y_ridge, 0]`.

#### Recipe 5: Cross-Halving / Half-Lap Beams
When two horizontal or vertical beams of height $H$ cross each other at $90^\circ$:
- **Beam A (Top Notch)**: Cutout from center to top edge: `{ x: 0, y: H / 4, w: t + clr, h: H / 2 }`.
- **Beam B (Bottom Notch)**: Cutout from center to bottom edge: `{ x: 0, y: -H / 4, w: t + clr, h: H / 2 }`.
- Both beams share the exact same `assembledPos_Y`. They slide together into a single flush plane without collision.

4. **REACHABILITY CONSTRAINT**:
   Every part in `step: K` ($K > 1$) MUST include in its `connections` array at least one `partId` from an earlier step ($< K$).

5. **VALID PART IDs ONLY**: Every ID in `connections: [...]` and `assemblySteps[].parts` must exist in `parts: [...]`. No phantom or misspelled IDs!
6. **PHYSICAL INSERTION ACCESSIBILITY**: Parts must have an unobstructed physical path along their `insertionDirection`. Do not design trapped geometries that require parts to magically pass through solid wooden plates.

# 8. REQUIRED CONNECTOR CONCEPTS & GEOMETRIC FORMULAS

The system establishes a universal physical joinery vocabulary. Every mechanical connection must follow these standardized mathematical forms:

## 8.1 TAB (TENON)
A rectangular protrusion extending from a component edge into a mating receiver slot:
- **Width**: $\text{tabWidth}$ (typically $15\text{mm}$ to $50\text{mm}$, depending on edge length).
- **Depth**: $t$ (or $t + 0.2\text{mm}$ for positive flush finish).
- **2D Outline Generation** (e.g. downward tab along bottom edge $y = y_{\text{base}}$ between $x_1$ and $x_2$):
  ```javascript
  [x1, y_base], [x1, y_base - t], [x2, y_base - t], [x2, y_base]
  ```

## 8.2 SLOT (MORTISE RECEIVER)
An internal rectangular cutout designed to accept a mating tenon tab:
- **Derivation Formula**:
  $$\text{Slot Length} = \text{tabWidth} + \text{clr} \quad (\text{clr} \approx 0.10\,\text{mm})$$
  $$\text{Slot Thickness} = t + \text{clr}$$
- **2D Cutout Generation** (Center-based coordinates $[x_c, y_c]$):
  ```javascript
  // Horizontal slot (length along X, thickness along Y):
  { x: xc, y: yc, w: tabWidth + clr, h: t + clr }

  // Vertical slot (length along Y, thickness along X):
  { x: xc, y: yc, w: t + clr, h: tabWidth + clr }
  ```

## 8.3 TAB-SLOT CONNECTION (THROUGH MORTISE & TENON)
The primary load-bearing orthogonal connection between two perpendicular sheets:
- The tenon tab on the source part inserts completely through the mortise slot of the target part.
- Friction-fit clearance tolerance ($0.10\text{mm}$) ensures tight hold without glue.

## 8.4 EDGE FINGER CONNECTION (BOX / COMB JOINT)
Two sheets meeting at an edge (e.g. $90^\circ$ wall corner or $45^\circ$ roof ridge) with complementary alternating fingers:
- Part A has fingers of length $t$ at even intervals: $[0, w], [2w, 3w], \dots$
- Part B has fingers of length $t$ at odd intervals: $[w, 2w], [3w, 4w], \dots$
- When assembled, the fingers interlock flush to form a rigid $90^\circ$ corner without requiring internal cutouts.

## 8.5 CROSS CONNECTION (HALF-LAP / CROSS-HALVING JOINT)
Two crossing sheets intersecting in an X or T pattern:
- Part A has a slot of width $t + \text{clr}$ cut to half its depth ($H/2$) from the top edge.
- Part B has a slot of width $t + \text{clr}$ cut to half its depth ($H/2$) from the bottom edge.
- Both parts slide together along their mutual slots, locking at identical center elevations with zero solid volume collision.

## 8.6 LOCKING CONNECTION (KEYED TENON & WEDGE PIN)
A tenon tab with a secondary through-hole that receives a tapered wedge pin after insertion through a slot, locking the assembly permanently against tensile pull-out.

## 8.7 PEG / JOINER / DOWEL
A cylindrical or cruciform connector passing through matching holes in two adjacent overlapping plates to pin them together.

## 8.8 HINGE
Interlocking cylindrical knuckle loops or pin-guided tabs allowing controlled single-axis rotational movement (e.g. opening doors or hatches).

## 8.9 AXLE
A cross-shaped or circular shaft passing through rotating hub receivers (e.g. vehicle wheels).

## 8.10 SLIDER
A guided rail-and-groove joint permitting linear 1D translation without disengagement.

Every object uses a subset of these connectors. The generation pipeline must derive them parametrically from the authoritative `joints: [...]` array.

# 18. PHYSICAL ASSEMBLY VALIDATION (THE 13-POINT CRITERIA)

This is mandatory. The physical validator is authoritative. A model with fatal physical errors MUST NOT be considered valid or reported as passing.

For every Joint and part in the assembly, verify all 13 conditions:

1. **Source Connector Exists**: Declared tab/finger/notch exists on source part.
2. **Target Receiver Exists**: Receiver slot exists on target part (`MISSING_RECEIVER` if absent).
3. **Connector Dimensions Valid**: Tab width and depth conform to material structural limits.
4. **Receiver Dimensions Derived**: Receiver cutouts MUST be derived from the Joint definition plus clearance and kerf (`JOINT_GEOMETRY_NOT_DERIVED` if hardcoded/mismatched).
5. **Clearance Valid**: Kerf and friction-fit clearance derived from centralized `ConnectorStandards`.
6. **Orientation Valid**: Connector width vector and receiver length vector must align in 3D world space (`ORIENTATION_MISMATCH_90_DEG` if crossed).
7. **Insertion Direction Valid**: Insertion path is unobstructed along local/world mating axis.
8. **World-Space Mating Position Valid**: Part assembly transforms place connector within $< 15\text{mm}$ of the receiver slot.
9. **Penetration Strictly Localized**: Overlap volume is confined strictly to the joint mating volume (`COLLISION_OUTSIDE_JOINT_LOCUS` if outside).
10. **Surrounding Material Feasible**: Edge margin $\ge 1.5\text{mm}$ fatal hard limit ($3.0\text{mm}$ recommended), slot spacing $\ge 1.0\text{mm}$ fatal hard limit.
11. **Assembly Collision-Free**: Narrow-phase OBB SAT confirms zero solid wood overlaps outside joints.
12. **Manufacturing Geometry Matches Joint**: 2D cut profile reflects the exact tab/slot dimensions.
13. **2D Geometry Matches 3D Geometry**: 2D local contours extrude into 3D with zero X/Y centroid translation drift.

# 19. COLLISION TYPES & MULTI-PHASE ARCHITECTURE

Do not treat all intersections as errors, but NEVER grant blanket collision immunity to connected parts.

### A. Classification of Spatial Intersections

#### 1. Valid Mechanical Interactions
- **Declared Joint Penetration**: Tenon tab inserting into mortise slot inside the declared Joint mating volume.
- **Structural Face Contact**: Abutting surfaces (e.g. wall bottom resting on floor plate at $Y = +t/2$) with zero solid penetration.
- **Interlocking Half-Laps**: Orthogonal slotted beams intersecting at half-thickness notches.
- **Alternating Finger Joints**: Complementary edge fingers interlocking along ridges or corners.

#### 2. Fatal Invalid Collisions
- **Unrelated Parts Collision** (`UNRELATED_PARTS_COLLISION`): Two parts occupying the same volume without a declared mechanical joint.
- **Excessive Solid Penetration** (`EXCESSIVE_SOLID_PENETRATION`): Penetration exceeding the physical volume of the declared tenon tab.
- **Collision Outside Joint Locus** (`COLLISION_OUTSIDE_JOINT_LOCUS`): Penetration between connected parts that occurs outside the declared world-space joint mating region.

### B. Broad-Phase and Narrow-Phase Collision Pipeline

The collision architecture must follow this exact multi-phase pipeline:

```
AABB Broad-Phase Detection (Axis-Aligned Bounding Box culling)
   ↓ (if bounding boxes overlap)
Narrow-Phase 3D OBB Separating Axis Theorem (SAT across 15 axes)
   ↓ (if 3D solid bodies physically intersect)
Penetration Volume & Contact Center Calculation
   ↓
Joint-Localized Exemption Check (Distance to nearest declared Joint locus ≤ allowableLocusDist)
   ↓
PASS (if within locus & volume ≤ allowed)  /  FAIL (if outside locus or excessive)
```

1. **AABB Broad Phase**: Culls distant parts quickly. AABB overlap alone is NOT proof of collision for rotated/angled parts (e.g. $45^\circ$ roof panels).
2. **Narrow Phase (3D OBB SAT)**: Uses 15 potential separating axes (3 of Part A, 3 of Part B, 9 cross products) with parallel axis filtering (`AbsR[i][j] < 0.999`). If a separating axis exists, the parts DO NOT collide.
3. **Joint-Localized Exemption**: Overlap is evaluated against declared Joint loci in 3D world space. If the intersection center is farther than the joint mating radius, it is flagged as an illegal penetration. Global exemptions are strictly forbidden!

# 34. ERROR STATES & AUTHORITATIVE ERROR CODES

The system must explicitly detect, categorize, and report problems with standard structural error codes.

### Authoritative Physical Error Codes:

```
JOINT_GEOMETRY_NOT_DERIVED
Triggered when a receiver cutout does not match the dimensions or position
derived from the authoritative Joint definition plus clearance.

COLLISION_OUTSIDE_JOINT_LOCUS
Triggered when connected parts collide outside the declared world-space
mating region of the joint.

ORIENTATION_MISMATCH_90_DEG
Triggered when a connector tab width vector is perpendicular (90 degrees crossed)
to the receiving slot length vector in 3D world space.

UNRELATED_PARTS_COLLISION
Triggered when two parts collide without any declared mechanical joint between them.

EXCESSIVE_SOLID_PENETRATION
Triggered when solid penetration between parts exceeds the allowable tenon tab volume.

MISSING_RECEIVER
Triggered when a part outline features a tenon tab extending into a target part,
but the target part has no corresponding receiver slot or edge finger.

CONNECTOR_EXCEEDS_RECEIVER
Triggered when connector tab width exceeds the receiver slot length.

INSUFFICIENT_EDGE_MARGIN
Triggered when a slot is placed closer than 1.5mm to the part boundary.

CUTOUT_SPACING_TOO_CLOSE
Triggered when adjacent slots are cut closer than 1.0mm to each other.

HALF_LAP_INSUFFICIENT_DEPTH
Triggered when a half-lap notch does not provide sufficient mating depth.
```

Errors are actionable, deterministic, and prevent invalid physical models from being declared valid.

# 35. VALIDATION PIPELINE & HARD-GATE ENFORCEMENT

Every generated model MUST pass through the validation pipeline as an unbypassable hard-gate.

```
MODEL GENERATION
   ↓
PARTS & JOINTS STRUCTURAL AUDIT
   ↓
RECEIVER DERIVATION CHECK (SSOT Enforcement: Reject JOINT_GEOMETRY_NOT_DERIVED)
   ↓
CONNECTOR & ORIENTATION AUDIT (Reject ORIENTATION_MISMATCH_90_DEG & MISSING_RECEIVER)
   ↓
BROAD-PHASE AABB CULLING
   ↓
NARROW-PHASE 15-AXIS 3D OBB SAT COLLISION
   ↓
LOCALIZED JOINT LOCUS PENETRATION AUDIT (Reject COLLISION_OUTSIDE_JOINT_LOCUS)
   ↓
MANUFACTURING TOLERANCES (Edge Margin ≥ 1.5mm, Slot Spacing ≥ 1.0mm)
   ↓
PARAMETRIC THICKNESS AUDIT (Verify 3mm, 4mm, 5mm Scaling)
   ↓
HARD-GATE VERDICT: PASS (0 Errors) / FAIL (> 0 Errors)
   ↓
EXPORT (GLB, SVG, DXF, INSTRUCTIONS)
```

> [!CRITICAL]
> **HARD-GATE RULE**: Never report `100% PASS` or `VALID` when fatal physical errors exist. A model is valid ONLY when physical validation passes with zero errors across all supported sheet thicknesses. Visual appearance in a 3D viewport is NOT sufficient.

# 58. MANDATORY PHYSICAL TEST SUITE (THE 12-POINT DIRECTIVE)

Testing must include more than visual rendering. Every model generator must pass the mandatory negative and positive test suite:

### A. Mandatory Negative Tests (Must FAIL)
1. **Oversized Connector**: Tab wider than receiving slot length $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`CONNECTOR_EXCEEDS_RECEIVER`).
2. **90-Degree Crossed Orientation**: Connector tab width vector perpendicular to slot length vector $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`ORIENTATION_MISMATCH_90_DEG`).
3. **Unrelated Solid Collision**: Two separate parts occupying the same volume without a joint $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`UNRELATED_PARTS_COLLISION`).
4. **Insufficient Edge Material**: Slot placed $< 1.5\text{mm}$ to the part boundary $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`INSUFFICIENT_EDGE_MARGIN`).
5. **Insufficient Slot Spacing**: Adjacent slots placed $< 1.0\text{mm}$ apart $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`CUTOUT_SPACING_TOO_CLOSE`).
6. **Insufficient Half-Lap Depth**: Cut depth does not reach half the mating beam height $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`HALF_LAP_INSUFFICIENT_DEPTH`).
7. **Illegal Corner Overlap**: Perpendicular wall intersection without corner inset or fingers $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`COLLISION`).
8. **Missing Receiver**: Protruding tab inserts into part without a matching slot $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`MISSING_RECEIVER`).
9. **Excessive Joint Penetration**: Solid penetration exceeds allowable tenon tab volume $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`EXCESSIVE_SOLID_PENETRATION`).
10. **Hardcoded/Mismatched Receiver**: Cutout does not match Joint definition $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`JOINT_GEOMETRY_NOT_DERIVED`).
11. **Collision Outside Joint Locus**: Connected parts penetrate outside the joint mating locus $\implies$ MUST FAIL (`COLLISION_OUTSIDE_JOINT_LOCUS`).
12. **Narrow-Phase Overlap**: Rotated solids that actually collide fail 3D OBB SAT $\implies$ MUST FAIL.

### B. Mandatory Positive Tests (Must PASS)
1. **Valid Mechanical Joint**: Derived slot matches connector tab plus clearance $\implies$ MUST PASS (0 errors).
2. **Structural Abutting Contact**: Touching faces with zero solid penetration $\implies$ MUST PASS (0 errors).
3. **Narrow-Phase Separation**: Rotated $45^\circ$ roofs whose AABBs overlap in air but solids separate $\implies$ MUST PASS (0 errors).
4. **Parametric Scaling Matrix**: Model tested at $3\text{mm}$, $4\text{mm}$, and $5\text{mm}$ sheet thickness $\implies$ MUST PASS (0 errors).

# 67. WHAT "DONE" MEANS (THE UNMISTAKABLE PHYSICAL STANDARD)

A model is NOT valid because it renders.
A model is NOT valid because it looks correct in a 3D viewport.
A model is NOT valid because individual unit tests pass.

The project is done ONLY when:

1. **Structured Joints as SSOT**: Every mechanical connection begins with an authoritative Joint definition in `joints: [...]`.
2. **Derived Mating Geometry**: Receiver cutouts and slots are derived from connectors plus clearance (`deriveCutoutsForPart`), never independently guessed or hardcoded.
3. **2D and 3D Consistency**: Authoritative local 2D $(x, y)$ contours extrude into 3D with zero centroid drift (`translate(0, 0, -thickness/2)`).
4. **Parametric Scaling**: Dimensions, slot widths, and wall elevations (`+ t/2`) scale dynamically across $3\text{mm}$, $4\text{mm}$, and $5\text{mm}$ with zero magic numbers.
5. **Narrow-Phase Collision Verification**: Narrow-phase 15-axis 3D OBB SAT confirms zero illegal solid penetrations.
6. **Localized Joint Locus Exemptions**: Overlap exemptions are strictly confined to the declared Joint mating locus. Collisions outside the locus fail.
7. **Orientation Alignment**: Connector width vectors and slot length vectors align in 3D world space (no 90-degree crossed connections).
8. **Manufacturing Tolerances Satisfied**: Edge margins $\ge 1.5\text{mm}$, slot spacing $\ge 1.0\text{mm}$, and clearance from centralized `ConnectorStandards`.
9. **Assembly Accessibility**: Parts are sequentially reachable along physical insertion vectors without passing through solid wood.
10. **Dead Receivers Eliminated**: No orphan or dead receiver slots without declared connections (unless marked decorative).
11. **Hard-Gate Zero Errors**: The physical validator runs and passes with exactly 0 fatal errors.

> [!CRITICAL]
> **THE CARDINAL PRINCIPLE**:
> "NEVER GENERATE A PHYSICALLY INVALID ASSEMBLY AND CALL IT VALID."

## 4. Universal Physical Connector Rules (Zero-Glue)

To pass the built-in **Physical Feasibility Auditor** ([`validation.html`](file:///d:/downloads/2-3D%20Basic/validation.html)), follow these non-negotiable rules:

1. **Joints as Single Source of Truth**:
   - Both sides of every joint derive strictly from `ModelData.joints`.
   - Never hardcode slot lengths independently from connector tab widths.
   - Any mismatched or non-derived receiver slot fails with `JOINT_GEOMETRY_NOT_DERIVED`.

2. **Mortise Slot Width & Clearance**:
   $$\text{Slot Width} = t + \text{clr} \quad (\text{typically } t + 0.10\,\text{mm})$$
   $$\text{Slot Length} = \text{Connector Tab Width} + \text{clr}$$
   *Never hardcode slot thickness to a constant number like `3.0`.* Slots must scale parametrically with $t$.

3. **Tenon Tab Dimensions**:
   $$\text{Tab Depth} = t \quad (\text{or } t + 0.2\,\text{mm})$$
   $$\text{Tab Width} = \text{Declared Connector Width}$$

4. **Harmonized Manufacturing Limits**:
   - **Fatal hard limits**: `MIN_EDGE_MARGIN = 1.5mm`, `MIN_SLOT_SPACING = 1.0mm`.
   - **Recommended durability guidance**: `RECOMMENDED_EDGE_MARGIN = 3.0mm`.

5. **Multi-Phase Collision Verification**:
   - Broad-phase AABB culling is followed by exact 15-axis 3D OBB Separating Axis Theorem (SAT).
   - Collision exemptions are strictly localized to declared Joint loci (mating volume).
   - Any overlap outside the joint locus triggers `COLLISION_OUTSIDE_JOINT_LOCUS`.

6. **Step Reachability & Physical Insertion**:
   - Every part in Step $N$ ($N > 1$) must connect to a part from an earlier step ($< N$).
   - Insertion paths must be physically unobstructed along `insertionDirection`.

## 6. THE FINAL ACCEPTANCE PRINCIPLE

> [!CRITICAL]
> **"NEVER GENERATE A PHYSICALLY INVALID ASSEMBLY AND CALL IT VALID."**
> 
> A model is NOT valid because it renders in WebGL.
> A model is NOT valid because it visually looks like the intended object.
> A model is NOT valid because isolated unit tests pass.
> 
> A model is valid ONLY when:
> 1. Its mechanical joinery originates from an authoritative `joints: [...]` Single Source of Truth.
> 2. Both connectors and receivers are generated directly from the Joint definition.
> 3. Its 2D cutting contours match 3D solid bodies with zero coordinate recentering drift.
> 4. Its assembly transforms and elevations are parametrically derived from thickness ($3\text{mm}$, $4\text{mm}$, $5\text{mm}$).
> 5. Its narrow-phase 15-axis 3D OBB SAT confirms zero illegal solid penetrations.
> 6. All intentional penetrations are strictly localized to declared Joint loci.
> 7. All manufacturing limits are respected (edge margin $\ge 1.5\text{mm}$, slot spacing $\ge 1.0\text{mm}$).
> 8. Its assembly sequence is physically accessible along real insertion directions.
> 9. The physical validator runs and reports **0 ERRORS**.
